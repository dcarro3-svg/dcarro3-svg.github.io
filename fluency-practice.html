Overview

Fluency frees the mind for harder things.

Working memory is limited. When a student has to stop and calculate 6 × 8 in the middle of solving a multi-step problem, that pause costs them the thread of their reasoning. Fluency practice addresses this directly.

The Fluency Practice System provides structured, targeted drilling of math fact families using spaced repetition and immediate feedback. Students work through fact sets in sequence, with missed facts cycling back until they're solid.

This isn't rote drilling for its own sake — it's precision preparation for the computation demands of the Groundwork curriculum's word problem work.

How It Works

01

Choose a Fact Set

Students select an operation and a number (e.g., ×7 or +8). The system presents all facts in that family in randomized order.

02

Practice with Cards

Each card shows the problem. Students recall the answer, then reveal it and mark whether they got it automatically or had to think.

03

Missed Facts Recycle

Facts marked "miss" re-enter the deck for the current session. The round ends when every fact has been answered correctly.

04

Track Session Progress

A progress bar and timer track each session. Students can see improvement across rounds as their response time drops.
