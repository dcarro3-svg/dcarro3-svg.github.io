About

Learning is structural.
So are good tools.

I spent years in classrooms watching students struggle — not because the content was too hard, but because the instruction wasn't built around how understanding actually forms. I started building tools to fix that.

Now I'm a graduate student in educational policy, and I build custom digital tools for educators who have a clear vision of what their students need but no way to make it. If you can describe the problem, I can build something that addresses it.

I work at discounted rates for educators and curriculum teams willing to let me add the project to my portfolio. Small scope, real impact, no risk.

Math Curriculum · Interactive Tool

Groundwork

A complete web-based curriculum for teaching relational understanding in mathematics. Four units covering simple, compound, and complex relations, word problems, math fact fluency, and computation — each with structured lessons, practice, and a bar model drawing tool. Built on the premise that comprehension is a teachable skill.

Self-Directed Learning · Visual Discrimination

Plant Family Identifier

A visual discrimination learning tool built to teach plant family identification through image-based practice. Designed as a self-study system using principles of perceptual learning — show, categorize, repeat. A prototype in instructional design for naturalist education.
