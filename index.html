About

Protect the teaching profession.

The teaching profession is undoubtedly going to change. As AI tools become more advanced, what it means to be a teacher is being questioned.

I believe that AI tools should place higher demand on human judgement, not less. I think that there is a way to use AI tools to augment our teaching abilities, rather than replace them.

I hope through partnerships with teachers and schools, Phototropic Learning and Design can help build a future where teachers are seen as the valued professionals they are.

Math Curriculum · Interactive Tool

Groundwork

A complete web-based curriculum for teaching relational understanding in mathematics. Four units covering simple, compound, and complex relations, word problems, math fact fluency, and computation — each with structured lessons, practice, and a bar model drawing tool. Built on the premise that comprehension is a teachable skill.

Math Curriculum · Structured Lessons

Relation Naming Lessons

Structured lessons for teaching students to identify and name the mathematical relations embedded in word problems. Covers simple, compound, and complex relations — building the comprehension skills that make computation meaningful.

Math Curriculum · Interactive Tool

Bar Model Drawing Tool

An interactive digital canvas for constructing bar models. Students drag and resize bars, label values, and build visual representations of word problem structures — turning abstract relations into concrete diagrams.

Math Curriculum · Fluency Practice

Fluency Practice System

A structured system for building math fact fluency. Students work through targeted fact sets with timed practice, immediate feedback, and spaced repetition — developing the automatic recall that frees working memory for problem-solving.

Math Curriculum · Progress Tracking

Progress Chart

A visual progress tracking tool that maps student advancement through the Groundwork curriculum. Teachers and students can see mastery by unit at a glance — making growth visible and helping identify where to focus next.
