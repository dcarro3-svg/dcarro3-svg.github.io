Overview

Know exactly where you are.

Curriculum only works when students and teachers can see where the learning is and where it isn't. The Progress Chart gives a clean, at-a-glance view of advancement through the Groundwork curriculum — by unit, by skill, by student.

Rather than a gradebook, this is a mastery map. It shows which units are complete, which are in progress, and which haven't been started — giving both students and teachers a clear picture of where to focus next.

The chart updates automatically as students complete lessons and practice sessions, with no manual entry required from the teacher.

What the Chart Tracks

Units

Unit Completion

Which of the four Groundwork units a student has worked through, and the percentage of lessons completed in each.

Mastery

Skill Mastery %

For each skill area — relation naming, bar modeling, fluency — the percentage of practice items answered correctly across all attempts.

Fluency

Fluency Session History

A record of each fluency practice session — fact set practiced, number of misses, and time taken — plotted over time.

Summary

At-a-Glance Dashboard

A single-screen summary for teachers showing every student's position in the curriculum — useful for grouping, conferencing, and intervention planning.

Designed for Both Sides of the Classroom

For Students

See your own momentum.

Students have a personal view of their progress chart — seeing exactly which units they've completed and what's left. Visible progress is motivating. It turns an abstract curriculum into a concrete path with a clear endpoint.

For Teachers

Know where everyone is, at once.

Teachers see a class-level dashboard — who is on track, who is stuck, and where the gaps are. No digging through gradebooks. The chart makes differentiation decisions obvious rather than guessed.
