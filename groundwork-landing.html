The Premise

Students don't fail math
because it's too hard.

They fail because no one taught them to read it. A word problem describes a situation with a relational structure. Before a student can solve it, they need to comprehend it. Relation naming is that comprehension step.

Groundwork builds that foundation — lessons, fluency drills, and a progress chart that makes growth visible, for every student, every session.
