Groundwork

Make learning make sense.

Practice the foundational skill of comprehension.

Comprehension in reading, conceptual understanding in math, these skills are often called higher-order thinking skills. They often aren't directly taught, but this leaves many students thinking they aren't smart, or aren't good at math. This program teaches the skill of comprehension in math and reading, by focusing on relational understanding.
