See It In Action

Every session
moves the line.

After each timed drill, the student's score is recorded to a progress chart automatically — no teacher input required. Rates are plotted on a semi-logarithmic scale so growth is visible from the very first session. Over days and weeks, the chart shows each fact family climbing toward its pass-off target. It's a concrete picture of fluency building in real time, session by session.
