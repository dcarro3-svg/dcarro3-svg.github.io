Overview

Fluency isn't just speed.
It's freedom.

When students have to stop and calculate 6 × 8 mid-problem, they lose the thread of their reasoning. Working memory is finite — every fact that isn't automatic is a tax on it. This system eliminates that tax.

The sequence moves from structured fact family instruction to timed fluency drills to applied double-digit computation. Each phase builds on the one before. Together, they develop the automatic recall the Groundwork curriculum depends on.

See It In Action

Every session
moves the line.

After each timed drill, the student's score is recorded to a progress chart automatically — no teacher input required. Rates are plotted on a semi-logarithmic scale so growth is visible from the very first session. Over days and weeks, the chart shows each fact family climbing toward its pass-off target. It's a concrete picture of fluency building in real time, session by session.
