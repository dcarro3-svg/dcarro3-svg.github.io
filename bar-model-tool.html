Overview

Make the invisible visible.

Bar models are one of the most powerful representations in mathematics education — but drawing them by hand is slow, imprecise, and hard to revise. This tool gives students an interactive canvas to build, label, and adjust bar models digitally.

Students construct models that match the structure of a word problem: a whole bar broken into parts, or two bars positioned for comparison. The act of building the model is itself the comprehension work.

The tool is designed for use alongside the Relation Naming Lessons — after students name the relation, they draw the model that represents it. Together, these two steps replace guessing with structured thinking.

Tool Features

Drawing

Drag-to-Build Bars

Students click to add bars and drag to resize them, snapping to a grid for clean proportional representation.

Labels

In-Place Labeling

Click any bar to type a value, variable, or "?" — labels stay anchored to the bar as it's resized.

Structure

Relation Templates

Pre-built templates for part-part-whole, comparison, and equal groups give students a starting structure to modify.

Output

Save & Export

Students can save their model as an image to include in written work or share with a teacher for feedback.
